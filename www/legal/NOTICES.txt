# Sources and verification record

This file records where the tables and values in the Component Marking Decoder were checked, and where a value could not be confirmed or differs between manufacturers. The audit was done in October 2026. If you find a mistake, please open an issue and cite the datasheet or standard.

**How to read the status column.** *Confirmed* means a published source agreed with the value. *Varies* means different manufacturers publish different values, and the tool says so. *Unverified* means I could not find a published source, so the tool either hedges the wording or leaves the entry out.

# Sources and constants

Every formula, table, and constant in the calculators, and where it comes from. Checked when the tool was written. If you find an error, please open an issue.

## Preferred-number series

E6, E12, E24, E48, and E96 follow IEC 60063. E48 is every other E96 value. The test suite checks the series lengths (6, 12, 24, 48, 96).

## Ohm's law and power

V = I × R, P = V × I = I²R = V²/R. Recommended resistor rating is the next standard size (1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 W) at least 2× the dissipation, a common 50 % derating rule of thumb.

## LED resistor

R = (Vs − n × Vf) ÷ I for LEDs in series, and one resistor per LED in parallel. Rounds up to the next standard value so current does not exceed the target.

## Voltage divider

Vout = Vin × R2 ÷ (R1 + R2). Output impedance is R1 ∥ R2. Loaded output replaces R2 with R2 ∥ Rload.

## Series and parallel

Series resistors and parallel capacitors add. Parallel resistors and series capacitors combine as 1 ÷ (1/a + 1/b + ...).

## RC filter and reactance

τ = RC, f_c = 1 ÷ (2πRC), 10 to 90 % rise time 2.2τ. X_L = 2πfL, X_C = 1 ÷ (2πfC), f₀ = 1 ÷ (2π√(LC)).

## 555 timer

Astable: f = 1.44 ÷ ((R1 + 2×R2) × C), high time 0.693 × (R1 + R2) × C, low time 0.693 × R2 × C. Monostable: T = 1.1 × R × C. Standard formulas from the 555 datasheets (Texas Instruments NE555, SE555).

## LM317

Vout = 1.25 V × (1 + R2 ÷ R1) + I_ADJ × R2, with a typical I_ADJ of 50 µA and about 3 V of dropout headroom. From the Texas Instruments LM317 datasheet.

## Wire gauge

Diameter = 0.127 mm × 92^((36 − n) ÷ 39) (ASTM B258). Resistance uses the resistivity of annealed copper, 1.724 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 20 °C (22 AWG works out to 0.6438 mm and about 52.96 Ω per km). Using 1.678 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m instead lowers results by about 3 %. This is not an ampacity table.

## I²C pull-up

Maximum: R ≤ t_r ÷ (0.8473 × C_b), where 0.8473 is the RC time from 30 % to 70 % of VDD. Minimum: R ≥ (VDD − V_OL) ÷ I_OL. Limits from the NXP I²C-bus specification (UM10204): t_r of 1000 / 300 / 120 ns, C_b of 400 / 400 / 550 pF, and I_OL of 3 / 3 / 20 mA for Standard, Fast, and Fast-mode Plus. V_OL is 0.4 V (0.2 × VDD when VDD is 2 V or below).

## Battery life

Average current = active × duty + sleep × (1 − duty). Run time = capacity × usable fraction ÷ average current. Self-discharge, temperature, and cut-off voltage are not modeled.

## BJT switch

Base current = load current ÷ forced beta (10 is the usual choice). Rb = (V_drive − V_BE) ÷ I_B, rounded down to an E24 value.

# Sources and constants

## ADC

Counts = Vin ÷ Vref × 2^n (floor), or ÷ (2^n − 1) with rounding. The Arduino reference documents `analogRead()` as 0 to 1023 on 10-bit boards. The ESP32 accuracy note (about 0.15 to 2.45 V at 11 dB attenuation) follows Espressif's ADC documentation. Check your chip revision.

## ADC divider

Vout = Vin × R2 ÷ (R1 + R2), searched over IEC 60063 E-series values. The AVR recommendation of about 10 kΩ or less source impedance is from the ATmega328P datasheet's ADC section.

## PWM

Period = 1 ÷ f, on time = duty × period, average = high level × duty. A first-order RC filter with cutoff f ÷ 10 attenuates the carrier by about 20 dB (single pole, one decade above the cutoff).

## Logic levels

Typical datasheet values, to be confirmed against your exact part:

| Family | Supply | VOH min | VOL max | VIH min | VIL max | Abs max input | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATmega328P | 5 V | 4.0 V | 0.9 V | 0.6 × Vcc = 3.0 V | 0.3 × Vcc = 1.5 V | Vcc + 0.5 V | Microchip AVR DC characteristics: VIL 0.3 × Vcc and VIH 0.6 × Vcc for Vcc 2.4 to 5.5 V; VOH and VOL at 20 mA, Vcc 5 V, 85 °C. Confirm in the ATmega328P datasheet. |
| ESP32 | 3.3 V | 0.8 × VDD = 2.64 V | 0.1 × VDD = 0.33 V | 0.75 × VDD = 2.475 V | 0.25 × VDD = 0.825 V | about 3.6 V (VDD + 0.3 V) | Espressif ESP32 datasheet, DC characteristics at VDD 3.3 V; pins are not 5 V tolerant |
| 5 V TTL | 5 V | 2.7 V | 0.4 V | 2.0 V | 0.8 V | 5.5 V | Standard TTL levels (74LS) |
| 74HCT | 5 V | 4.4 V | 0.1 V | 2.0 V | 0.8 V | Vcc + 0.5 V | TTL-compatible CMOS inputs |
| 74HC / CD4000 | 5 V | 4.4 V | 0.1 V | 3.5 V | 1.5 V | Vcc + 0.5 V | CMOS thresholds at 5 V |
| 3.3 V LVTTL | 3.3 V | 2.4 V | 0.4 V | 2.0 V | 0.8 V | 3.6 V | JEDEC LVTTL levels |

Verdict rules: danger if the driver's supply exceeds the receiver's maximum input; level shifter needed if the high or low margin is negative; marginal under 0.2 V of margin; otherwise compatible.

## UART (AVR)

UBRR = fclk ÷ (16 × baud) − 1 in normal mode, fclk ÷ (8 × baud) − 1 in double-speed mode, rounded; actual baud = fclk ÷ (16 × (UBRR + 1)). From the ATmega328P datasheet, USART section. At 16 MHz, 115200 baud gives −3.5 % error in normal mode and +2.1 % with U2X.

## AVR timers (CTC mode)

f = fclk ÷ (prescaler × (OCR + 1)). Prescalers: Timer0 and Timer1 1, 8, 64, 256, 1024; Timer2 1, 8, 32, 64, 128, 256, 1024. Timer0 and Timer2 are 8-bit (OCR up to 255), Timer1 is 16-bit (up to 65535). ATmega328P datasheet.

# Sources and constants


## Linear regulator thermal

Power = (Vin − Vout) × I + Vin × Iq. Efficiency = Vout × I ÷ (Vin × (I + Iq)). Junction temperature = ambient + P × θJA. Required heatsink θSA = (Tjmax − Ta) ÷ P − θJC − θCS. Standard thermal-resistance model (junction, case, sink, ambient), as described in regulator datasheets (for example the LM317 and 78xx families).

## Capacitor discharge

V(t) = V0 × e^(−t ÷ RC), so t = RC × ln(V0 ÷ V). Energy = ½CV². Standard RC discharge.

## Battery pack

Series adds voltage, parallel adds capacity; energy = nominal voltage × Ah; current = C-rate × capacity. Typical per-cell voltages: Li-ion/LiPo 3.7 / 4.2 / 3.0 V; LiFePO4 3.2 / 3.65 / 2.5 V; NiMH 1.2 / 1.45 / 1.0 V; alkaline 1.5 / 1.6 / 0.9 V; lead-acid 2.0 / 2.35 / 1.75 V per cell. These are typical figures to be confirmed against the cell datasheet.

## Battery life

Average current = (Ia × ta + Is × ts) ÷ (ta + ts). Switching regulator: Ibat = Iload × Vout ÷ (Vbat × efficiency) + Iq. Linear regulator: Ibat = Iload + Iq.

## Filter capacitor

Ripple ≈ I ÷ (f × C), with f equal to twice the mains frequency for full-wave rectification. Standard capacitor-input filter approximation.

# Sources and constants


## Trace width

IPC-2221: I = k × ΔT^0.44 × A^0.725, with I in amps, ΔT in °C, A in square mils, k = 0.048 (external) or 0.024 (internal). Copper weight: 1 oz = 1.378 mil (35 µm). IPC-2152 is the newer standard and may give different results for a given board. The IPC-2221 curves cover up to about 35 A.

## Trace resistance

R = ρ × L ÷ (w × t), copper ρ = 1.724 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 20 °C, temperature coefficient 0.00393 per °C.

## Via current

Barrel cross-section = π ÷ 4 × (D² − (D − 2t)²), then the IPC-2221 internal conductor formula with k = 0.024. This is an estimate; published via data is limited.

## Value converter and frequency

Engineering prefixes (SI): giga 10⁹ down to pico 10⁻¹². Wavelength = c × velocity factor ÷ f, with c = 299,792,458 m/s.

## Number bases

Standard positional notation; signed values use two's complement for 8, 16, and 32 bits.

## E-series

IEC 60063 preferred numbers: E6, E12, E24, E48, and E96. E48 is every other E96 value.

## Resistor color code, capacitor codes

IEC 60062 color code and marking codes. Temperature coefficient colors: brown 100, red 50, orange 15, yellow 25, blue 10, violet 5 ppm/°C (black 250 on some parts).

## I²C addresses

The usual 7-bit addresses for common breakout boards, collected from the chip datasheets and the common board documentation. Jumpers and variants change them. To be confirmed with a scanner and your board.

## AWG

Diameter = 0.127 mm × 92^((36 − n) ÷ 39) (ASTM B258). Copper resistivity 1.724 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 20 °C.

## Logic levels

Typical datasheet values, as listed in the Embedded Systems Toolkit sources.
